O namaAbout us

Tradicija na tanjiruTradition on a plate

✦

Čevabdžinica Željo je godinama poznata adresa za sve koji traže autentičan sarajevski ćevap. Meso pečemo na žaru po narudžbi, a služimo ga uz topli somun, sirovi luk, jogurt i kajmak, onako kako to Baščaršija zna.For years Čevabdžinica Željo has been a well-known address for anyone seeking an authentic Sarajevo ćevap. Our meat is grilled to order and served with warm somun, raw onions, yogurt and kajmak, the way Baščaršija does it best.

  • Sočno meso, savršeno pečeno na žaruJuicy meat, perfectly grilled
  • Brza usluga i izdašne porcijeFast service and generous portions
  • Pristupačne cijene, oko 10–15 KM po osobiFair prices, around 10–15 KM per person
